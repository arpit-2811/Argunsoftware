What is Argun Garage Management Software?

Argun Garage Management Software is a cloud-based, GST-compliant platform built specifically for Indian automobile workshops and insurance bodyshops.

It digitises the entire repair lifecycle — from vehicle arrival and digital job card creation to surveyor pre-invoices, parts inventory tracking, and final GST tax invoices. Everything runs in any browser with zero installation required.

Complete Module Breakdown (17 Modules)

Comprehensive capabilities included in Argun Garage Management Software.

MODULE 01

Job Card Management

Create digital job cards for every vehicle upon arrival. Track repair jobs through 7 status stages: New Inquiry → Pre-Invoice Sent → Estimate Sent → Insurance Approved → In Repair → Ready for Delivery → Delivered.

  • Supports 3 claim types: Cashless, Reimbursement, Cash
  • Records vehicle make, model, registration number, fuel type, & odometer
  • Captures insurer company, policy type (Zero Dep / Comprehensive), claim number, surveyor name
  • Tracks arrival mode (Self-Drive, Towing, Crane) & survey status
  • One-click WhatsApp share of job card receipt to customer
MODULE 02

Insurer Pre-Invoice

Generate detailed pre-invoices to send to insurance surveyors before work begins, speeding up survey approval times.

  • Line-item parts list with quantities, unit prices, GST rates, and HSN codes
  • Labour charges as separate line items with discount support
  • Auto CGST+SGST or IGST calculation based on insurer GSTIN state code
  • Coverage flag: mark each part/labour as covered by Insurer or Customer
  • Insurer Payable vs. Customer Payable summary with PDF download
MODULE 03

Insurance Repair Estimate

Formal repair estimates for cashless and reimbursement insurance jobs with full tax breakdown.

  • Damage item list with Part Number, Name, Qty, Rate, Discount, & GST
  • Bill-to / Ship-to party support for insurance billing
  • GST deduction & approved amount tracking
  • Advance payment and balance due tracking
  • Professional PDF with embedded UPI payment QR code
MODULE 04

Official GST Tax Invoice

Fully GST-compliant tax invoices structured into Table A (Parts) and Table B (Labour).

  • Sequential auto-numbering
  • Workshop GSTIN auto-filled from settings
  • Customer GSTIN support for B2B invoices
  • CGST / SGST / IGST auto-calculated per line item
  • UPI QR Code auto-embedded in invoice PDF for fast payment
MODULE 05

Inventory & Parts Management

Full spare parts catalogue with real-time stock tracking and low-stock alerts.

  • Part Number, Name, Category, HSN Code, GST Rate
  • Categories: Body Parts, Electrical, Engine, Interior, Consumables, Others
  • Buy Price and Sell Price with auto margin calculation
  • Low stock threshold alerts on dashboard
  • Barcode label generation (EAN/Code128) & storage location tagging
MODULE 06

Stock Ledger

Complete inward/outward stock movement ledger for every spare part.

  • Transaction Types: Purchase, Job Usage, Opening Balance, Adjustment
  • Full audit trail recorded with date and user ID
  • Per-part date-wise ledger view & real-time closing balance
MODULE 07

Purchase Management

Record purchase bills from spare part suppliers with multi-part entries.

  • Multi-part entries in a single purchase bill
  • GST-inclusive or GST-exclusive price support
  • Supplier-wise purchase history and invoice tracking
MODULE 08

Supplier Management

Maintain supplier directory with full payables ledger.

  • Supplier Master: Name, GSTIN, Phone, Address, State
  • Supplier-wise transaction ledger & balance due tracking
MODULE 09

Customer Management

Centralized customer database with complete vehicle repair history.

  • Customer master with contact details
  • Customer-wise transaction history & receivables ledger
  • Outstanding balance tracking
MODULE 10

Insurance & Policy Renewal Tracker

Manage insurance company masters and generate automated policy renewal reminders.

  • Insurance Company directory with GSTIN & state code
  • Policy Renewal Alert Engine: notify customers when insurance is expiring
  • WhatsApp policy renewal reminder templates

MODULE 11

Expense Tracking

Record all daily workshop operational expenses.

  • Categories: Rent, Electricity, Staff Wages, Tools, Consumables, Others

MODULE 12

GST Sales Reports

Automated GST sales reporting for tax reconciliation.

WhatsApp Integration

Garage Management Software for Indian Workshops

Argun Garage Management Software (ArgunGMS) is a cloud-based workshop management system for automobile garages, multi-brand service centres, and insurance authorised bodyshops in India. Unlike generic ERP systems, ArgunGMS is built around Indian auto repair workflows — from the way insurance claims are processed by Indian insurers to the GST compliance requirements of a workshop billing system.

For Insurance Authorised Bodyshops

Cashless insurance repair shops deal with surveyor assessments, pre-approval letters, insurer invoices, and delivery challans. ArgunGMS automates this entire documentation chain. Garages can raise insurer pre-invoices directly from the job card, track claim approval status, and generate final insurer invoices in the exact format required by major Indian insurance companies.

For Multi-Brand Service Centres

Multi-brand service centres benefit from ArgunGMS's capacity management, technician allocation, and bay tracking features. The software ensures no vehicle is missed, every job card is closed, and outstanding payments are followed up through WhatsApp reminders.
