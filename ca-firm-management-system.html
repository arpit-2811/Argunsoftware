CA Firm Management Software — Built for Chartered Accountants

Argun CA Firm Management System is a comprehensive practice management software designed exclusively for Chartered Accountant firms operating in India. It addresses the unique challenges of CA practice — managing hundreds of clients, tracking multiple compliance deadlines, coordinating staff assignments, and maintaining client communication — all within one integrated platform.

Task and Deadline Management for CA Firms

CA firms in India operate on a complex statutory calendar — GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, ITR filing deadlines, TDS return due dates, ROC annual filings, and more. Argun CA Firm Management System auto-populates this compliance calendar and creates client-specific task lists with deadlines. Staff members receive task notifications and clients receive automatic WhatsApp reminders when documents are needed.

Client Communication and Document Management

Clients frequently delay document submission, which causes last-minute filing rushes. Argun CA Firm Management System solves this by sending automated, scheduled WhatsApp messages to each client based on what documents are outstanding.
