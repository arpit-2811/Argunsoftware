Overview

Argun Loan Management System (ArgunLMS) is engineered to simplify loan disbursement, borrower KYC recordkeeping, interest calculation, daily collections, and overdue tracking for lending agencies and micro-finance organizations.

It replaces prone manual ledger registers with automated installment schedules, instant payment receipts, and real-time portfolio health reports accessible from any browser.

Core Capabilities & Modules

Key tools included in Argun Loan Management System.

Borrower & Loan Account Management

Maintain comprehensive borrower KYC records, guarantor profiles, loan principal amounts, interest rates, and customized repayment frequencies.

  • Borrower KYC document storage & contact details
  • Guarantor details & loan agreement attachment
  • Customizable start dates & loan tenure configuration

EMI & Interest Computation Engine

Automated interest calculation supporting multiple interest computation formulas.

  • Flat interest and reducing balance calculations

Daily & Monthly Collection Reports

  • Instant payment receipt generation via PDF
  • WhatsApp & SMS due reminders to borrowers
  • Overdue notice printing and logging

Detailed Financial Analytics

Portfolio health dashboards providing bird's-eye visibility over outstanding capital, interest earned, and non-performing loans.

How Argun Loan Management System Works

From borrower registration to final repayment — fully automated.

1

Register Borrower & Disburse Loan

Create a complete borrower profile with KYC documents, guarantor details, and photograph. Set loan amount, interest rate, repayment schedule, and disburse with one click.

2

Track EMI & Collections

The system auto-generates an EMI schedule for each borrower. Field agents record daily, weekly, or monthly collections. Overdue reminders are sent automatically via SMS or WhatsApp.

3

Reports & Portfolio Monitoring

View total outstanding portfolio, NPA accounts, collection efficiency, and agent-wise performance — all in real-time. Generate borrower ledger statements and interest receipts on demand.

Loan Management Software for Indian Lenders

Argun Loan Management System (ArgunLMS) is a cloud-based lending software for money lenders, NBFCs, cooperative societies, SHG lenders, and micro-finance institutions operating in India. It digitises the entire loan lifecycle — from borrower registration and loan disbursement to EMI collection, interest calculation, and portfolio reporting — eliminating the need for manual registers and spreadsheets.

For Money Lenders and Private Financiers

Private money lenders in Bihar and across India face challenges in tracking multiple borrowers, managing daily collection routes, and maintaining proper interest accounts. ArgunLMS provides a structured borrower database with KYC documents, automated repayment schedules, and collection tracking that gives lenders complete visibility into their portfolio without complex accounting knowledge.

For NBFC and Micro-Finance Companies

NBFC companies and micro-finance institutions have regulatory reporting requirements alongside operational loan management needs. ArgunLMS supports multiple loan products, customer group lending, joint liability group tracking, and generates the portfolio reports required for internal audits and compliance.
